# 🍷 Wine of Time

**Wine of Time** is a personal website built with **React**, designed as a digital space to share thoughts, projects, and stories over time. The site combines a clean, modern interface with a calm, reflective aesthetic, making it easy for visitors to explore blogs, learn about projects, and get in touch.

---

## 🌐 Live Website

👉 **Live Demo:**  https://wineoftime.vercel.app/

---

## ✨ Sections & Features

### 📝 Blogs
- Collection of written posts and reflections  
- Clean reading experience with structured layouts  
- Easy navigation between posts  

### 🚀 Projects
- Showcases personal and professional projects  
- Each project includes descriptions and relevant links  
- Designed to highlight work clearly and effectively  

### 👤 About
- Introduces the creator and the purpose of the website  
- Shares background, interests, and journey  

### 📬 Contact
- Contact form or direct links for communication  
- Simple and accessible way for visitors to reach out
